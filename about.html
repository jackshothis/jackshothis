Hi, I'm Jack — welcome to Jack Shot This.

I'm a photographer and videographer based in Vancouver, BC. I've been shooting for over 5 years, and I enjoy working on all kinds of projects. From portraits and landscapes to real estate, events, architecture, sports, and product photography, I like keeping things varied and never really limiting myself to one style.

One of the things I enjoy most about this work is that every project is different. Shooting different subjects and working in different environments gives me the chance to learn new techniques, experiment, and keep improving. I don't like sticking to just one look or approach. I adapt each shoot to what works best for the project, whether that's something natural and simple or a little more cinematic.

When I'm not behind a camera, I enjoy spending time at church, where I help out with various ministries and events. Outside of that, I love travelling, hiking, and exploring new places. I'm also always up for a good drive, a bit of go-karting, or finding somewhere new to eat. I like keeping busy, trying new things, and making the most of my time!

At the end of the day, I just love creating, learning, meeting great people, and capturing things worth looking back on.
